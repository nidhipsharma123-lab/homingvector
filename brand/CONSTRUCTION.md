# HOMINGVECTOR — mark construction

Generated by `tools/make_brand.py` from one set of constants. Edit the constants there, never the SVGs.

## The idea
"Homing" is convergence onto a point; "vector" is direction with magnitude. The mark is the **V of
VECTOR, broken**: a heavy stroke drives into the vertex, a hairline races toward it and stops short,
and a single reticle corner closes under the gap. The lock is formed by what is **absent** — the eye
completes the vertex the hairline never reaches. Motion arriving at certainty.

## Grid (48 × 48 units)
| Element | Geometry | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy stroke | `M3 5 H13 L25 34 H19 Z` — 10 u wide at the top, 6 u at the foot | The single heavy weight. It *arrives*: it narrows as it drives down. |
| Hairline | top edge x 43–45.5, ends at y 29 (2.5 u display, 4 u etch) | Weight contrast 4:1 against the heavy stroke. Ends 5 u above the heavy stroke's foot: the V never closes. |
| Lock corner | `M27 39.5 H36.5 V32 H39 V42 H27 Z` (2.5 u; 4 u etch) | One corner, not four. Four ticks is the most overused defense mark alive. Signal red. |
| Angles | heavy stroke 67.5° from horizontal, hairline 56°, vertex gap ≈ 56° | Nothing softer than 30°. No arc, round join or round cap exists in any file. |
| Asymmetry | left stroke 4× the right; the lock sits right of centre, under the hairline's gap | Symmetry reads corporate. Asymmetry reads as intent and direction. |

## Wordmark
Stroked centre-lines on a 24 u cap height, 3.4 u stroke, 3.2 u tracking. Curves are replaced by
**5 u chamfers** (O, G, C, R) — the same cut the mark uses. The **V is the mark's broken V** at
wordmark scale, not a font glyph. M alone uses a bevel join: its 27° centre vertex would otherwise
mitre 7 u through the baseline.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Clear space: **12 u of the 48 u mark grid** (¼ of the mark's height) on every side, lockup included.
- Mark minimum: **16 px** using `logo-etch.svg` / `favicon.svg` (4 u hairline and lock). Below 24 px
  never use the 2.5 u display weights — the hairline drops below one device pixel.
- Lockup minimum: **96 px wide**. Below that use the mark alone.

## Legibility proof at 16 px
`brand/favicon-16.png` is rasterised by the same headless Chrome the verify suite uses. At 16 px the
heavy stroke is 3 px, the hairline 1 px and the lock corner a 1 px L in lime: all three survive, and
the silhouette reads as a V with a lock. Re-run `tools/make_icons.sh` after any geometry change.

## Colour
Mark in bone `#ECE7DA` on slate `#0B1417`; the lock corner in **vector lime `#C6F032`** (14.1 : 1 on the
ground). Lime means "live, tracking, chosen" across the whole identity; alarm red `#FF4A2B` is never used
in the mark. Mono variants use one colour for all three parts. No gradients, shadows, bevels or
extrusion, ever. (Until 2026-09-17 the lock was red; changed so the identity does not read as a
black-and-white-plus-red copy of anyone else's.)

## Misuse
- Do not close the V, extend the hairline to the vertex, or add the missing three corners.
- Do not round anything, outline the mark, or place it on an image without a solid ground.
- Do not recolour the heavy stroke lime; lime belongs to the lock alone. Never use alarm red in the mark.
- Do not add a turtle, shield, crosshair circle, globe or animal. The products are named Turtle;
  the company is not.
