TurtleShield

The AI we’re building. Runs on every vehicle.

TurtleShield is the AI at the centre of Homingvector. Every drone already flies itself; what breaks under jamming is the team. TurtleShield takes the team out of the base station and puts it on every vehicle.

Keeps the drones you already own.

It sits on top of the autopilot. Adding a second autopilot brand changed zero files in our core. That brand, ArduPilot, has only been tested standing still so far.

Air, ground and water, together.

Fixed-wing aircraft, ground robots and surface boats share one plan and hand work to each other.

Built for the edge.

Designed for an NVIDIA Jetson Orin next to a Cube Orange flight controller, with no base station in the loop.

Suggests. Never decides.

It can spot something, keep track of it and suggest a way to intercept. A person makes the call. There is no weapon code.

Turtle Eyes

Ground control station (GCS) for TurtleShield.

Turtle Eyes is the GCS operators use to run a TurtleShield team. Three modes, one click each. Orders go in as plain English. Nothing moves until a person holds the button down.

    We haven’t flown a real aircraft yet. Everything on this page comes from simulation, or from a real flight controller wired to a simulator. You should hear that from us before anything else.
